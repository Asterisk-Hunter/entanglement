Why outputs converge

Read overlap by magnitude. |overlap| rises 0.50 → 0.875 → 0.969 → 0.992. The signed overlap is retained in the technical figure.

Exact values

At d = 256, the executable win probability is about 0.508. With 4000 trials, that gap cannot be resolved from chance. d = 1024 is theory only.
