# Brag Plan: Entangle Lab

## What is this app?
An interactive browser lab that lets beginners explore singlet-state correlations and run a finite-shot CHSH Bell test.

## The angle
Make a counterintuitive result tangible: individual outcomes look random, while the shared pattern can cross the classical Bell bound.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on the real lab and land the line “Two particles. One strange pattern.” over the angle controls.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Alice and Bob set analyzer angles in the live app.
- One click reveals a single opposite-spin pair.
- A 2,600-pair-per-setting run lands at |S| = 2.806, above the classical limit of 2.
- The field guide connects the result to the underlying probability model.

## Outro / punchline
“Quantum theory, one browser, zero lab coats required.” End on the Entangle Lab identity and field-guide link.

## User flow worth showing
Open the lab → measure one pair → run CHSH → inspect the guide.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: polished science short, confident and curious, without hype.
- Interpretation: Spacious framing and deliberate motion let the real experiment carry the story.

## Format: landscape: 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0b0e19
- Accent: #b99cff
- Text: #f2f0ff
- Display font: Manrope
- Body font: Manrope
- Strongest visual element: the violet-on-ink CHSH lab interface and live S-value.

## Share copy (draft)
Two particles. One strange pattern. Explore the singlet state and run a Bell test in Entangle Lab.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 12 by Sascha Ende, ENDE.APP, CC BY 4.0.
- Music treatment: fade up over 0.8s, stay low under narration, lift gently at the measured result, and fade out over the final 1.2s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset; target 8.74s and 17.47s for the result and closing reveal.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; a violet halo follows the music's bass energy, without waveform graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, with a quiet measurement click and one soft score reveal accent.
- Audio-coupled moments: analyzer angle changes, measurement result, and Bell score reveal.
- Restraint rule: narration stays clear; no dramatic impacts over the science.
