03 THE IDEA

Not a message.
A relationship.

Entanglement does not let Alice send information to Bob. Each local outcome is unpredictable. The striking part appears only when they later compare their records.

01
A···B

Prepare a pair

The singlet state is a shared two-qubit state that cannot be split into independent states for Alice and Bob.

02

Choose directions

Each observer picks a measurement axis. A setting is chosen independently for each side.

03
+−+

Compare statistics

One pair tells little. Many pairs reveal correlations that no local hidden-variable model can reproduce for the right settings.
